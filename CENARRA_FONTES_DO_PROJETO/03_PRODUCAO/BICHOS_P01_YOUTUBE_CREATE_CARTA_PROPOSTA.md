## Clipe 06 — Téo descobre a carta

Usar Téo examina a folha do envelope.png. Usar 8 s na montagem. Esta é a descoberta da demissão e antecede o momento em que ele lê a assinatura.

```text
Vertical 9:16 cinematic 3D shot. Match the uploaded photo: Téo, the gray cat in a navy suit and burgundy tie, alone at the walnut desk. Keep his face, outfit and warm window light. The gold trophy stays on the right. Fixed camera. 0–2 s: he slides the half-exposed sheet out of the open cream envelope and holds it below his chin, blank back toward camera. 2–6.5 s: his eyes scan the page, then widen. His ears drop. Only Téo speaks, in native Brazilian Portuguese, low and increasingly alarmed. Say exactly once: “Ué... tem uma carta de demissão aqui. Com o meu nome.” Pause before the last sentence. 6.5–8 s: mouth closed, he lifts his eyes toward someone offscreen, frozen. Quiet office ambience. No extra voices, words, music, captions or readable text.
```

## Clipe 02 — Bela pergunta

Usar Bela pronta para a pergunta.png. Esta é uma alternativa de teste para a tomada falada que falhou no Flow. Se passar no QA, usar os primeiros 4 s na montagem de 60 s, incluindo a pergunta inteira e a reação. Se faltar uma saída limpa, a [montagem com o envelope em silêncio](BICHOS_P01_CLIPE02_CAPCUT_CONTINGENCIA_PROPOSTA.md) continua disponível.

```text
Vertical 9:16 cinematic 3D close-up. Match the uploaded photo: Bela, the caramel dog with floppy ears and a green blazer, alone at the walnut desk. Preserve her face, outfit and warm window light. Fixed camera. The sealed cream envelope stays still. Bela raises one eyebrow and gives a pointed sideways look. Start speaking in the first half-second. Only Bela speaks, with a natural adult female voice in Brazilian Portuguese. Say exactly once: “Você abriu o envelope ou só viu o troféu?” Sharpen the irony on the last words. Finish by 3.5 s, close her mouth and hold the stare. Quiet office ambience. No other voices, extra words, music or captions.
```
